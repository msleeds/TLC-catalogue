Sophie Frances Leeds
The Story

A studio built on honest country

MS Leeds Studio began with a simple idea: that the light, land and people of Western Queensland deserved to be photographed without pretence, and printed to a standard that would outlast a passing trend.

Sophie works between Charleville and the surrounding districts, photographing the paddocks, homesteads, rivers and skies that make up the South-West — and, through Bugalugs, the weddings and gatherings where those same communities come together.

Every print that leaves the studio is made by hand: numbered, signed and logged in the studio register, with a certificate of authenticity to match. Editions are strictly limited. Once they're gone, they're gone.

Brand Philosophy

Rooted in Australian stoicism

Rooted in Australian stoicism, we believe in focus, presence and gratitude. As the light moves across us, we honour fleeting moments and turn them into something worth keeping.

From Field To Frame

The Process

  1. 01
    In the field. Photographs are made on location across Western and South-West Queensland, in natural light, without staging.
  2. 02
    Selection. Only a small number of images are chosen for print each season — held to the same restrained eye across every collection.
  3. 03
    Archival printing. Each work is printed as an archival pigment print on 300gsm cotton rag stock.
  4. 04
    Pencil editioning. Every print is numbered and signed by hand — an edition of 25, and no more.
  5. 05
    Finishing. Blind emboss and letterpress detailing mark each parcel with the studio hallmark.
  6. 06
    Certificate & despatch. A signed certificate of authenticity accompanies every print, logged against its edition number.
