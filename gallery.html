Signature Edition

Limited to 25 · never reprinted

  • 300gsm cotton rag, large format
  • Numbered and signed by hand in pencil
  • Signed certificate, logged in the studio register
  • Blind emboss & letterpress finishing
  • Signature images are never sold as open editions
