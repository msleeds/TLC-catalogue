The Studio

Sophie Frances Leeds

MS Leeds Studio is the work of photographer Sophie Frances Leeds, based in Charleville in South-West Queensland. The studio's fine art prints are drawn from the paddocks, homesteads and open skies of the Western Queensland country — honest photographs, made without pretence, and finished to a standard worth holding onto.

Every print leaves the studio numbered, signed and logged. Editions are sealed — when they're gone, they're gone.

Materials & Process

Honest materials, held to museum standard

The collector feel comes from restraint — textured cotton rag, a pencil in a human hand, one considered accent per print. Nothing shiny that isn't earned.

01

Cotton Rag, 300gsm

Archival pigment on cotton rag stock, printed for longevity, not just for now.

02

Pencil Editioning

Each print is numbered and signed by hand — 25 exist, then never again.

03

Certificate of Authenticity

Every edition is accompanied by a signed certificate and logged in the studio register.

04

Blind Emboss & Letterpress

Finishing marks pressed, not printed — the studio hallmark on every parcel.
